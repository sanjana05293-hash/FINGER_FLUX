How to use

  1. Click Enable camera and allow access.
  2. Raise one hand with your index finger pointing up.
  3. Move your fingertip. Effects follow it.
  4. Pick Air Drawing to write; lines stay until you press Clear canvas.

Only your index fingertip is tracked. Letter recognition and extra gestures are not part of this version.

Privacy: the camera starts only after you click. Frames are analysed in your browser and are never recorded, saved or uploaded. No microphone is used.

Downloaded from public CDNs: Google Fonts, the MediaPipe Tasks Vision script and WASM files (jsDelivr), and the hand-landmark model (Google storage). An internet connection is needed on first load.
